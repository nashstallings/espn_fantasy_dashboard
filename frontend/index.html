ESPN has no "sign in with ESPN" for third-party apps, so this connects using the two session cookies your own browser already holds. Nothing here creates an account and no password is ever stored.

Where do I find these cookies?
  1. Log in at fantasy.espn.com in your browser.
  2. Open developer tools (F12) → Application → Cookies → https://fantasy.espn.com.
  3. Copy the values of SWID and espn_s2.
  4. Paste them above. They are validated against ESPN before anything is saved.

These are live ESPN session credentials. Only paste them into software you trust, and use Disconnect here (or log out of ESPN) to invalidate them.
